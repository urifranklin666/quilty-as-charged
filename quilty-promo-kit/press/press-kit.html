The story

Quilters spend a surprising amount of time doing arithmetic. How many 3½" squares come out of a width of fabric? How much backing for a queen? What size do I cut the squares for a four-inch flying goose? It is the least interesting part of a craft that is otherwise entirely about colour and texture, and it is usually done on the back of an envelope in a fabric shop.

Quilty as Charged moves that arithmetic into your pocket. Its centrepiece is a pixel designer: paint a quilt square by square on a grid up to 24 × 24, using twelve named fabrics, with mirror and four-way medallion symmetry to keep things even. Underneath the grid, a cutting chart updates as you paint — how many squares of each fabric, how many width-of-fabric strips that needs, and how much yardage to buy, rounded up to the nearest eighth the way it is sold.

Around that sit the calculations quilters repeat forever: half-square and quarter-square triangles, no-waste flying geese, borders, sashing, binding, backing panels, standard quilt and batting sizes, and a cheat sheet for the fat-quarter-to-honey-bun end of the fabric shop. There is a colour studio with a wheel, harmonies and saveable palettes you can pass to a friend as a short text code.

The whole app is a single self-contained page in a native Android shell. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is tracked, and every feature works with the aeroplane mode on.

Features

  • Pixel quilt designer — grids 8 × 8 to 24 × 24, twelve fabrics, four symmetry modes
  • Live cutting chart — squares, WOF strips and ⅛-yard yardage per fabric
  • Seven block presets — nine patch, checkerboard, sawtooth star, pinwheel, hourglass, log cabin, diamond medallion
  • Photo import — matches an image to your fabric palette on device
  • Sewing Basket — save, reload and edit designs
  • Colour studio — wheel, lightness, harmonies, five preset palettes, shareable custom palettes
  • Patch math — cut size, square count, yardage, estimated cost
  • Triangle math — HSTs, QSTs, no-waste flying geese
  • Backing & binding — strips, panels, borders, sashing
  • Reference — quilt and batting sizes, pre-cut cheat sheet, nine quilting tips
  • Pattern export — printable grid + cutting list via the Android share sheet
Assumptions the maths uses: 42" usable width of fabric · ¼" seam allowance on every side (cut size = finished + ½") · yardage rounded up to the nearest ⅛ yard · 4" backing overage per side · 12" extra binding for corners and joins.
