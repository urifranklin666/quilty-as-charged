Quilty as Charged

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026 · Applies to the Android app com.quiltyascharged.app

The short version

Quilty as Charged does not collect anything about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. Your quilt designs and colour palettes are stored on your own device and are never uploaded anywhere.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves the following in your device's local browser storage, inside the app's private sandbox:

  • Quilt designs you save to your Sewing Basket (a name, a grid size, and the colours of each square)
  • Colour palettes you save
  • Custom colours you have mixed in the Colour Studio

This data never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Network connections

The app makes one kind of outbound request, and only when you have a connection:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) — the app loads its two display typefaces from Google's font service. As with any web request, Google receives your device's IP address and general request information. Google describes this at developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy. No information about you or your designs is included.

If there is no connection, the app falls back to your device's built-in fonts and every feature continues to work normally. No other servers are contacted, and nothing is ever sent to the developer.

Permissions

The app declares a single Android permission, INTERNET, which is what allows the font request above. It does not request access to your location, camera, contacts, microphone, or device identifiers.

When you choose Import image, your phone's own file picker is used, and the picture is processed on the device to match it to fabric colours. The image is not uploaded and is not retained by the app.

When you choose Export pattern, the app writes a PNG into its own private cache folder and hands it to the Android share sheet, so you can decide where it goes. The app itself sends it nowhere.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
