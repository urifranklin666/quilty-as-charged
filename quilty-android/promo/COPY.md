### Full description (4000 char limit — this draft is ~2,150)

```text
Quilty as Charged 🧵 — the quilting toolkit that does the fabric math for you.

Paint a quilt square by square, and watch the cutting chart write itself: how many squares of each fabric, how many width-of-fabric strips, and how much yardage to buy. No spreadsheets, no scribbled envelopes, no standing in the shop doing sums.

🎨 PIXEL → QUILT DESIGNER
• Paint on a grid from 8 × 8 up to 24 × 24 with a finger or stylus
• Twelve named fabrics — muslin, cherry, rose, butter, pumpkin, sage, pine, denim, midnight, plum, cocoa and thread
• Symmetry modes: free stitch, mirror ↔, mirror ↕, and 4-way medallion
• Seven classic block presets: nine patch, checkerboard, sawtooth star, pinwheel, hourglass, log cabin and diamond medallion
• Scrappy shuffle for instant inspiration, eyedropper to re-pick a fabric, and undo when the rotary cutter slips
• Import a photo and have it matched to your fabrics automatically
• Save designs to your Sewing Basket and reload them any time
• Export a printable pattern sheet — grid plus cutting list — straight to the Android share sheet

📋 CUTTING CHART THAT WRITES ITSELF
Every design gets a live chart: finished quilt size, cut size including ¼" seam allowance, and for each fabric the number of squares, the number of width-of-fabric strips, and the yardage rounded to the nearest ⅛ yard.

🎨 COLOUR STUDIO
Spin a full colour wheel, adjust lightness, and pull complementary, triadic and analogous harmonies. Five ready-made palettes — Autumn Harvest, Berry Patch, Ocean Tide, Vintage Feedsack and Amish Nights. Save your own palettes, and share them with a quilting friend as a short text code.

🧮 PATCH MATH
Enter your finished square size and quilt top dimensions to get cut size, squares needed, squares per yard, total yardage — and an estimated fabric cost from your price per yard.

📐 TRIANGLE MATH
Half-square triangles, quarter-square triangles and no-waste flying geese, with the cut sizes worked out before you touch the fabric.

📏 BORDERS, BACKING & BINDING
Binding strip counts and yardage, optional borders and sashing, and how many backing panels you need — with the usual 4" overage per side already allowed for.

🛏️ SIZE CHART
Quilt top and batting dimensions for baby/crib, throw, twin, full, queen and king.

✂️ PRE-CUT CHEAT SHEET
Fat quarters, fat eighths, jelly rolls, charm packs, layer cakes, mini charms and honey buns — what they measure and what they are good for.

🧵 A STITCH OF WISDOM
Nine bits of practical advice on pressing, seam accuracy, starch and chain piecing.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking, analytics or in-app purchases. Your designs and palettes never leave your phone. Everything except the display fonts works with no connection at all.

Made for quilters who would rather be sewing than doing arithmetic.
```

## 8. Claims checklist ✅

Before publishing, confirm each of these still matches the code:

- ✅ Grid sizes 8, 10, 12, 16, 20, 24 — `pxsize` options
- ✅ 12 named fabrics — `FABRICS`
- ✅ 4 symmetry modes — `pxsym` options
- ✅ 7 block presets — `pxpreset` options / `applyPreset`
- ✅ 5 preset palettes — `PALETTES`
- ✅ 6 quilt sizes, baby to king — `QUILT_SIZES`
- ✅ 7 pre-cuts — `PRECUTS`
- ✅ 9 tips — `TIPS`
- ✅ Yardage rounds up to the nearest ⅛ yard — `toYards`
- ✅ Cut size = finished + ½" (¼" seam each side) — `renderLegend`, `renderMath`
- ✅ 42" width of fabric assumed — `WOF`
- ✅ Backing allows 4" overage per side, binding adds 12" — `renderBB`
- ⚠️ "Works offline" — true for all features, **but** the app fetches display fonts from
  `fonts.googleapis.com` on launch. See `README.md`, "One thing to fix before you publish".
